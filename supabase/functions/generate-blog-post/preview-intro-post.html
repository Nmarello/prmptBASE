The vault is open.

We built prmptVAULT because generating AI images and video shouldn't require a PhD in prompt engineering.

prmptVAULT Team · March 19, 2026 · 4 min read

Today, prmptVAULT is open to the world.

If you've spent any time with AI image and video generators, you know the drill. Blank text box. Stare at it. Type something. Get something back that's almost right but not quite. Tweak the prompt from scratch. Repeat. Lose your last good prompt somewhere in a browser tab you'll never find again.

We got tired of that workflow. So we built something better.

What prmptVAULT is

prmptVAULT is an AI image and video generation platform built around one idea: your prompts should work for you, not against you.

Instead of a blank text box, you get structured prompt templates — fields for subject, style, lighting, mood, camera angle. Fill in what matters, skip what doesn't. The model gets cleaner instructions. You get better outputs. Faster.

Everything you generate gets saved to your library automatically. Every prompt, every image, every video. It's there when you come back. No copying and pasting into Notion. No mystery files on your desktop.

The models

We've pulled together the best image and video generation models available right now — all in one place. Here's a taste of what's inside.

Flux Pro — Image

Flux Pro from Black Forest Labs is one of the sharpest image models available. High resolution, strong prompt adherence, coherent detail across the whole frame. This is the model you reach for when quality matters.

Nano Banana 2 — Image

Nano Banana 2 produces a distinct, stylized look that stands apart from photorealistic models. Rich color, strong composition, immediately recognizable. Great for creative work where you want something that doesn't look like every other AI image.

Minimax Video — Video

Minimax Video turns text prompts into smooth, cinematic video clips. Motion is fluid, scenes hold together. This is text-to-video that actually looks like something you'd use.

Luna Dream Machine — Video

Luna Dream Machine from Luma AI pushes video quality further — longer clips, more dynamic motion, better scene consistency. If you're generating video for real use, this is the one.

These are just four. There are dozens more inside, and new ones drop every week.

Who it's for

Designers who need reference imagery fast. Marketers building campaigns. Developers prototyping interfaces. Creators who want to produce more without spending half their day fighting with prompts.

You don't need to know how to write prompts. That's the point. The structure does that work for you.
