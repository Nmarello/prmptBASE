Meet Flux 1.1 Pro

The fastest high-res image model we've added yet — and it shows.

prmptVAULT Team · March 19, 2026 · 3 min read

Flux 1.1 Pro is now live on prmptVAULT. It's fast, sharp, and doesn't need hand-holding to produce something worth keeping.

What Flux 1.1 Pro does

It generates high-resolution images from text prompts. Up to 2048px on the long edge. The outputs are clean — good lighting, coherent structure, detail that holds up when you zoom in.

It's built on the Flux architecture from Black Forest Labs. This version trades some of the flexibility of the base model for speed and consistency. You get results in 3–5 seconds.

What makes it good

Most models at this speed sacrifice detail. Flux 1.1 Pro doesn't. Faces are coherent. Backgrounds don't fall apart at the edges. Typography in images is still broken — but that's every model right now.

  • Consistent lighting across complex scenes
  • Strong prompt adherence — what you write is what you get
  • Fast enough to iterate without waiting

How to use it on prmptVAULT

Open the vault, pick Flux 1.1 Pro from the model list. The prompt form is structured — subject, style, lighting, mood. Fill in what matters, leave the rest blank. It'll generate and save straight to your library.

If you want to iterate, your last prompt is always right there. Tweak one field, regenerate. No copy-pasting between sessions.
